## Course Description

Research is increasingly conducted in environments shaped by artificial
intelligence, computational data, and rapidly
changing analytical tools.

This course introduces you to quantitative research design and
data analysis while examining how large language models and related artificial
intelligence systems can support, transform, and complicate the research
process.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of the course, you will be able to:

1. Distinguish research methods from analytical techniques.
2. Translate substantive questions into measurable variables.
3. Design surveys, experiments, and quasi-experimental studies.
4. Develop a reproducible LLM-assisted information-extraction procedure.
5. Use Python to prepare, analyze, and visualize data.
6. Conduct and interpret regression, ANOVA, and chi-square analyses.
7. Apply and evaluate computational text-classification approaches.
8. Use AI coding tools while maintaining human oversight.
9. Evaluate validity, reliability, privacy, ethics, and reproducibility.
10. Communicate findings to technical and nontechnical audiences.

## Technology

You will use:

- Google Colab
- Python libraries, including pandas, NumPy, SciPy, statsmodels, and Matplotlib
- Hugging Face
- GitHub
- Approved large language model tools

No prior programming experience is required.

Specific chapters and sections will be assigned on the relevant weekly course pages. Students are not expected to read any of the listed books in full.
