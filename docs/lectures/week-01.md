# Week 1: Research Methods in the Age of AI

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

1. Distinguish research methods from analysis techniques.
2. Describe how AI may enter different stages of research.
3. Explain the basic purpose of tokens, embeddings, and transformers.
4. Identify potential benefits and risks of LLM-assisted research.
