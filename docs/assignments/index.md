# Assignments

These public pages provide an overview of the major learning activities and preparation materials for the course.

| Assignment                                   | What You Do                                                                                                                                                                                      | What It Demonstrates                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Colab Access Check**                       | Set up access to Google Colab and confirm that you can open and use a computational notebook.                                                                                                    | Readiness to work in a cloud-based research environment                                        |
| **Human + AI Literature Screening Exercise** | Work with a research question, identify peer-reviewed studies, make your own relevance judgments, and compare those judgments with classifications produced by a pretrained language model.      | Human oversight, literature evaluation, AI-assisted research, and model validation             |
| **Block 1 Knowledge Assessment**             | Demonstrate understanding of modern research tools, foundational LLM concepts, computational environments, basic Python interpretation, and limitations of AI-assisted workflows.                | Conceptual understanding of contemporary computational and AI-enabled research                 |
| **Research Design Brief**                    | Develop a concise research proposal that connects a research question or hypothesis to scholarly literature, sampling, measurement, and an appropriate research design.                          | Research design reasoning, methodological justification, and critical consideration of AI use  |
| **Final Project Analysis Plan**              | Work with an instructor-provided dataset and research question to identify relevant variables, select an analytical method, and justify the proposed workflow.                                   | Analytical planning and the ability to connect research questions, data, and methods           |
| **Final Stakeholder Research Presentation**  | Conduct an analysis in Python/Google Colab, interpret the results, identify limitations, reflect on any AI-assisted coding or debugging, and communicate the findings to a stakeholder audience. | Data analysis, interpretation, responsible AI use, reproducibility, and research communication |

---

## Assignment Philosophy

The assignments are designed to move progressively from:

**tool literacy → research design → analysis → interpretation and communication**

Across the course, the emphasis is not simply on whether a computational or AI tool produces an answer. You should also be able to explain:

* why a research approach is appropriate;
* what the computational or AI tool is doing;
* how you verified the output;
* what limitations remain; and
* what the evidence means for the research question.
